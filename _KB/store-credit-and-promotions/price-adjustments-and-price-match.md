# Price Adjustments and Price Match

**Summary.** If an item's price drops within **7–14 days** of purchase, {{STORE_NAME}} refunds the
**difference** once (excludes clearance/flash sales). Price **matching** against other retailers, if
offered, requires an identical in-stock item from an eligible retailer with proof.

## Rule details
- **Price adjustment:** same item drops within **7–14 days** → refund the difference once, to the
  original method ([REF-005](../refunds/partial-refunds-and-deductions.md)); excludes clearance/flash
  sales.
- **Price match (if offered):** identical, in-stock item from an eligible retailer, with a
  link/screenshot; excludes marketplaces, used items, and mispriced listings.
- Cross-reference [PAY-006](../payments-and-billing/price-adjustments.md) for the billing mechanics.

## Examples
1. $50 item bought 3 days ago now $40, not clearance → refund **$10**.
2. Flash-sale item dropped further → excluded.
3. Competitor lists the identical item cheaper, in stock → check eligibility, then match if offered.

## What the agent must not do
- Do not adjust clearance/flash-sale prices.
- Do not match marketplace/used/mispriced listings.

## Escalation
Match eligibility disputes, or adjustments on orders over $100 → escalate.

## Customer-facing wording
> "Since the price dropped within our adjustment window and it isn't a clearance item, I can refund
> the $10 difference to your original payment method."
