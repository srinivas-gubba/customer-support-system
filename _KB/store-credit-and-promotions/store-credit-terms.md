# Store Credit Terms

**Summary.** Store credit is a {{STORE_NAME}} balance (e.g., from a goodwill delay credit or a gift
return). It is **not redeemable for cash**, applies at checkout like a payment method, and expires
**12 months** after issuance. Store credit may **not** replace a legally required refund.

## Rule details
- Not redeemable for cash; applied at checkout against the order total.
- Expiry: **12 months** from issuance (configurable; disclose it).
- IF the law requires a refund (e.g., FTC cancellation of an unshipped order) → a refund to the
  **original payment method** is owed, **not** store credit ([LEGAL-001](../legal/compliance-notes.md), [DELAY-001](../delays-and-compensation/delay-compensation-tiers.md)).
- Distinct from a **gift card** ([PROMO-004](gift-cards.md)).

## Examples
1. $15 goodwill delay credit → usable at next checkout; expires in 12 months.
2. "Can I cash out my store credit?" → No; it can be spent in full on any order.
3. Unshipped order cancelled → refund to card, not store credit.

## What the agent must not do
- Do not offer cash for store credit.
- Do not substitute store credit for a required refund.

## Escalation
Expired-credit reinstatement requests, or disputes → escalate.

## Customer-facing wording
> "Your store credit of ${amount} is on your account and applies automatically at checkout. It's
> valid for 12 months and can't be cashed out, but you can use the full amount on any order."
