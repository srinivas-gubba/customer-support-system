# Kitchen Accessories

**Summary.** Kitchen accessories follow the standard **30-day** window and must be **unused and in
original packaging**. **Used food-contact items** (opened and used) may be refused on hygiene grounds.
Defective or damaged items are covered as usual.

## Rule details
- Unused, original packaging → returnable ([RET-003](../returns/return-condition-requirements.md)).
- **Used food-contact** items (e.g., used cookware/utensils) → may be refused (hygiene, [RET-002](../returns/non-returnable-items.md)).
- Damaged/defective on arrival → [DMG-001](../damaged-or-wrong-items/damaged-item-claims.md).

## Examples
1. Unused, boxed utensil set, change of mind → returnable.
2. Used, washed frying pan, change of mind → may be refused (hygiene/condition).
3. Blender arrived cracked → damaged-item remedy.

## What the agent must not do
- Do not accept used food-contact items as full-refund change-of-mind returns.
- Do not deny a genuine damaged/defective remedy.

## Escalation
Disputed condition, or value over $100 → escalate.

## Customer-facing wording
> "Kitchen items can be returned within 30 days if they're unused and in the original packaging. Once
> used, food-contact items usually can't be returned — but if it arrived damaged, I'll sort that out."
