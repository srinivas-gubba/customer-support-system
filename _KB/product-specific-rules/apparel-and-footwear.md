# Apparel and Footwear

**Summary.** Clothing and shoes are returnable within 30 days **unworn, with tags attached** and
(for shoes) in the **original box**. **Swimwear/intimates without an intact hygiene liner are
non-returnable.** Size/fit issues are best handled as **exchanges**.

## Rule details
- Unworn, unwashed, **tags attached**; shoes in original box, no outsole wear ([RET-003](../returns/return-condition-requirements.md)).
- Swimwear/intimates without a hygiene liner → **non-returnable** ([RET-002](../returns/non-returnable-items.md)).
- Wrong size/fit → **exchange** is usually fastest ([RET-006](../returns/exchanges.md)).

## Examples
1. Unworn dress with tags, 10 days → returnable or exchange.
2. Shoes worn outside, scuffed soles → may be refused or partially refunded.
3. Swimwear with the liner removed → non-returnable (hygiene).

## What the agent must not do
- Do not accept worn/washed apparel or shoes with outsole wear as a full-refund return.
- Do not accept liner-removed swimwear/intimates.

## Escalation
Disputed condition, or value over $100 → escalate.

## Customer-facing wording
> "Clothing can be returned within 30 days if it's unworn with tags attached. For a size swap, an
> exchange is usually quickest — want me to set that up?"
