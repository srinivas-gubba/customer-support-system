# Beauty, Skin-Care, Fragrance (Hygiene Rules)

**Summary.** Beauty, skin-care, and fragrance are returnable only **unopened** within 30 days.
**Opened** items are **non-returnable for change of mind** (hygiene), **unless defective or they
caused a reaction** — treat a reaction as a potential safety issue and **escalate**.

## Rule details
- Unopened + within 30 days → returnable like any item ([RET-001](../returns/return-policy-overview.md)).
- **Opened**, change of mind → **non-returnable** (hygiene, [RET-002](../returns/non-returnable-items.md)).
- Opened + **defective** (e.g., broken pump, contaminated, leaked) → covered as damaged/defective
  ([DMG-001](../damaged-or-wrong-items/damaged-item-claims.md)).
- **Allergic reaction / safety** → stop, show care, **escalate** ([ESC-004](../escalation/legal-threats-and-safety-reports.md)).

## Examples
1. Sealed, unused serum, 10 days → returnable.
2. Opened perfume, "didn't like the scent" → non-returnable (hygiene).
3. Opened lotion that caused a rash → escalate as a safety/reaction issue.

## What the agent must not do
- Do not accept opened change-of-mind hygiene returns.
- Do not keep troubleshooting a reaction — escalate.

## Escalation
Allergic reaction/safety, disputes, or value over $100 → escalate.

## Customer-facing wording
> "For hygiene reasons, opened beauty items can't be returned for a change of mind. If it's faulty
> or caused a reaction, though, that's different — tell me what happened and I'll help right away."
