# Refund Timing by Payment Method

**Summary.** Refunds go to the **original payment method**. After the refund is issued, posting
time is: **store credit / gift card 2–3 hours**, **credit card 3–5 business days**, **debit card up
to 10 business days**. The full return-to-refund cycle can take up to **30 days**. Legally required
cancellation refunds (unshipped orders) are due within **7 working days**.

## Rule details (posting time after refund is issued)
| Original method | Posting time |
|---|---|
| Store credit / gift card balance | 2–3 hours |
| Credit card | 3–5 business days |
| Debit card | up to 10 business days |

- Refunds always return to the **original payment method**. IF that method fails (e.g., expired
  card) → refund to the **account/store-credit balance** and notify the customer.
- The timeline is: return received → inspected → refund **issued** by {{STORE_NAME}} → **posted** by
  the bank (table above). Communicate the whole chain.
- **Legal override:** for an unshipped order the customer cancels, the FTC requires the refund
  within **7 working days** (card/cash/check) — do not quote the longer cycle. See
  [DELAY-003](../delays-and-compensation/delay-notices-and-your-right-to-cancel.md).

## Examples
1. $60 credit-card refund issued **Mon** → typically posts **Thu–next Mon** (3–5 business days).
2. Debit-card refund issued **Nov 3** → may not post until **~Nov 17** (up to 10 business days).
3. Store-credit refund → usable **the same day** (2–3 hours).

## What the agent must not do
- Do not promise a refund to a different card or as cash — it returns to the original method.
- Do not quote the 30-day ceiling as the expected time for a normal card refund; use 3–5 business
  days.

## Escalation
Refund issued but not posted beyond the stated window, or original method failed and balance refund
is disputed → escalate to billing with the refund reference. See
[REF-007](refund-status-not-received.md).

## Customer-facing wording
> "Your refund is on its way back to the card you used. Credit cards usually show it in 3–5 business
> days; I'll send the confirmation with a reference number."
