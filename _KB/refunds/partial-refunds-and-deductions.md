# Partial Refunds and Deductions

**Summary.** A refund equals the **item price paid after discounts**. It **excludes original
shipping** (unless the store is at fault) and may be reduced for **return shipping on change-of-mind
returns**, a **restocking fee on opened electronics**, or **missing/used parts**. Promotional
certificate value is never refunded as cash.

## What is and isn't included
- **Included:** item price actually paid (after any discount).
- **Excluded by default:** original (outbound) shipping — refunded only when the store is at fault.
- **Possible deductions:**
  - Change-of-mind **return shipping** → see [RET-005](../returns/return-shipping-costs.md).
  - **Restocking fee** (15% on opened electronics only) → see [RET-009](../returns/restocking-fees.md).
  - **Missing parts/accessories** or reduced condition.
  - **Promo proration:** if a returned item drops the order below a promo threshold, the discount is
    prorated → see [PROMO-002](../store-credit-and-promotions/promo-code-rules.md).
- **Promotional certificate value is not refunded as cash** — it is not money the customer paid.

## Examples
1. $50 item bought with a $10 promo, change of mind, $6 return shipping → refund = $50 − $10 promo
   − $6 shipping = **$34**.
2. Opened $200 laptop (store at fault? no — change of mind) → 15% restocking = $30 → refund
   **$170** (escalate: over $100).
3. Item returned missing its charger → deduct the charger's value or request it; explain the
   reduced refund up front.

## What the agent must not do
- Do not apply an **undisclosed** deduction — state every deduction **before** the customer returns.
- Do not deduct return shipping or restocking when the **store is at fault** (damaged/wrong/defective).
- Do not refund promotional-certificate value as cash.

## Escalation
Disputed deduction, or a deduction that wasn't disclosed before the return, or total over $100 →
escalate.

## Customer-facing wording
> "Here's the breakdown for order #{id}: item $50, minus the $10 promo applied and $6 return
> shipping, so your refund is $34. Want me to go ahead?"
