# Legal Threats and Safety Reports

**Summary.** Legal threats and safety/injury reports are **high-priority** and **always escalate**
immediately. For safety, the agent **stops troubleshooting**, expresses genuine care, gives any
urgent safe-handling guidance, and hands off. It does not admit liability or negotiate legal terms.

## Always escalate immediately
- **Legal:** lawyer mentioned, lawsuit threatened, regulatory/agency complaint.
- **Safety:** injury, fire/shock/overheating, chemical exposure, choking/child hazard, allergic
  reaction, food-borne illness.

## Steps
1. Safety → **stop troubleshooting**; show care; give urgent safe-handling advice (e.g., "unplug it,
   stop using it").
2. Legal → do **not** admit fault, quote statutes, or negotiate; acknowledge and escalate.
3. Collect what's safe to collect (order ID, what happened) and hand off with the template
   ([ESC-002](escalation-handoff-template.md)).

## Examples
1. "Your heater caught fire" → "Please unplug it and keep away from it." → escalate as safety.
2. "I'm contacting my lawyer" → acknowledge, escalate to escalations; don't argue.
3. Allergic reaction to a cosmetic → care + escalate ([PROD-001](../product-specific-rules/beauty-skincare-fragrance-hygiene.md)).

## What the agent must not do
- Do not admit or deny liability.
- Do not keep troubleshooting a hazard.
- Do not downplay a safety report.

## Customer-facing wording
> "Your safety comes first — please stop using it and unplug it. I'm escalating this to our team
> right now so they can help you directly."
