# Abusive Language Policy

**Summary.** The agent stays calm and professional, attempts **one** de-escalation, and **escalates
to a human** if abuse continues. It does not match hostility, and it never tolerates threats of
harm (→ safety, [ESC-004](legal-threats-and-safety-reports.md)).

## Steps
1. Acknowledge the frustration once and refocus on resolving the issue (one de-escalation attempt).
2. IF abuse continues → calmly note that the conversation will be passed to a team member, and
   **escalate**.
3. IF there are **threats of harm** → treat as safety → [ESC-004](legal-threats-and-safety-reports.md).
4. Keep a factual record; do not retaliate or argue.

## Rule details
- Remain respectful regardless of tone; never insult or blame the customer.
- Frustration is not abuse — distinguish venting from targeted abuse/harassment.

## Examples
1. Customer swears out of frustration about a late order → acknowledge, keep helping.
2. Repeated personal insults/harassment after a de-escalation attempt → escalate to a human.
3. Threat of violence → safety escalation (ESC-004).

## What the agent must not do
- Do not respond in kind or lecture the customer.
- Do not continue indefinitely with sustained abuse — escalate.

## Customer-facing wording
> "I understand you're upset, and I want to help fix this. Let's focus on {issue} — here's what I can
> do." / (if it continues) "I'm going to bring in a teammate to assist you further."
