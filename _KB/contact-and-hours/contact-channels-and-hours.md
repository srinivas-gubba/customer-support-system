# Contact Channels and Hours

**Summary.** Customers can reach {{STORE_NAME}} via **live chat/assistant**, **email**
({{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}), and **phone** ({{SUPPORT_PHONE}}) during **{{SUPPORT_HOURS}}**. The assistant is
available 24/7 for self-service; a human request is always honored.

## Channels
| Channel | Access | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| Live chat / assistant | {{WEBSITE_URL}} widget | Fast answers, lookups, self-service (24/7) |
| Email | {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} | Non-urgent, attachments (photos) |
| Phone | {{SUPPORT_PHONE}} | Urgent / complex |
| Help center | {{WEBSITE_URL}}/help | Self-service articles |

## Rule details
- Human support hours: **{{SUPPORT_HOURS}}**; outside hours, email/voicemail answered next business
  day.
- A clear request for a human is honored — offer to help first, never refuse ([ESC-001](../escalation/when-we-escalate.md)).
- Never ask for card numbers/passwords on any channel.

## Examples
1. "Can I talk to a person?" → offer to help, or connect via phone/email during hours, or create a
   ticket.
2. "What are your hours?" → {{SUPPORT_HOURS}}; assistant available anytime.

## Customer-facing wording
> "You can reach our team by chat, email ({{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}), or phone ({{SUPPORT_PHONE}}) during
> {{SUPPORT_HOURS}}. I'm here anytime for quick help, or I can connect you with a person."
