# Response-Time Commitments

**Summary.** Live chat/assistant replies in **minutes**; email within **1 business day**; escalations
are followed up within **{{SLA}}**. The assistant sets a specific expectation whenever it hands off
or promises a follow-up.

## Targets
| Channel / path | First response target |
|---|---|
| Live chat / assistant | Minutes |
| Email | Within 1 business day |
| Phone | During {{SUPPORT_HOURS}} |
| Escalation follow-up | Within {{SLA}} |

## Rule details
- Always give a concrete timeframe on a handoff ([ESC-002](../escalation/escalation-handoff-template.md)).
- Don't promise a resolution time the team can't meet; commit to a **follow-up** time instead.

## Examples
1. Escalating a $140 damage claim → "You'll hear back within {{SLA}}."
2. Emailed question → "We reply within 1 business day."

## Customer-facing wording
> "I've escalated this and you'll get a reply within {{SLA}}. If you email us, we respond within one
> business day."
