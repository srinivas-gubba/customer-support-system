# Holiday Hours

**Summary.** Human support hours are reduced or closed on US public holidays; the assistant remains
available 24/7 for self-service. During peak season, replies and shipping may run slower, and the
**holiday return extension** ([RET-008](../returns/holiday-return-extension.md)) applies.

## Rule details
- On US public holidays, phone/email may be **closed**; the assistant still handles self-service.
- Peak-season volume can slow responses and shipping ([DELAY-004](../delays-and-compensation/carrier-delays-weather-and-peak-season.md)).
- Keep the specific closed dates current each year in [policy configuration].

## Examples
1. Message on a public holiday → assistant helps now; human follow-up next business day.
2. Peak-season delay → set honest expectations + apply goodwill delay credit if late.

## Customer-facing wording
> "Our team is off for the holiday today, but I can help you right now. For anything needing a person,
> they'll follow up the next business day."
