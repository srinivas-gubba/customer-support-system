# Processing Times

**Summary.** Orders are prepared and dispatched within **1–2 business days** before transit begins.
The delivery estimate the customer sees already includes processing. Processing is paused only by a
payment hold or a stock issue.

## Rule details
- Processing time: **1–2 business days** (Mon–Fri, excluding US holidays).
- Total delivery = processing (1–2 business days) + transit ([SHIP-001](shipping-methods-and-costs.md)).
- A payment hold or out-of-stock line can delay processing → check order status
  ([ORD-005](../order-management/order-status-definitions.md)).

## Examples
1. Order placed Friday afternoon → processing counts from the next business day (Monday).
2. Standard order placed Monday → dispatched by Tue–Wed, then 3–7 business days transit.

## What the agent must not do
- Do not tell a customer an order is "late" during the normal 1–2 day processing window.
- Do not promise same-day dispatch unless the store offers it.

## Escalation
Processing stalled well past 2 business days with no payment/stock reason → escalate to fulfillment.

## Customer-facing wording
> "Orders are prepared within 1–2 business days, then the carrier transit time applies on top. For
> order #{id}, it's still within the normal processing window."
