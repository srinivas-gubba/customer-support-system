# Unordered or Extra Items (Keep as a Gift)

**Summary.** If a customer receives **merchandise they never ordered**, US law (39 U.S.C. § 3009)
lets them **keep it as a gift**. {{STORE_NAME}} does **not** demand its return. If we shipped an
extra item in error, we may **politely ask** and offer a prepaid label — never require it.

## Rule details
- IF the customer received an item they **did not order** → they may **keep it**; do not bill them or
  demand return.
- IF we shipped an **extra** item by mistake → we may **ask** for it back and provide a **prepaid
  label**, framing it as optional.
- Distinguish from a **wrong item** (they ordered A, got B) → that's [DMG-002](../damaged-or-wrong-items/wrong-item-received.md)
  and we send the correct item.
- Legal basis: [LEGAL-001](../legal/compliance-notes.md).

## Examples
1. Customer got a bonus item they didn't order → "It's yours to keep."
2. We accidentally shipped two of an item, billed for one → ask politely with a prepaid label;
   accept "no."
3. They ordered a blue shirt and got a red one → **wrong item** (DMG-002), not this.

## What the agent must not do
- Do not demand return of or charge for unordered merchandise.
- Do not confuse an unordered extra with a wrong-item substitution.

## Escalation
High-value mis-ships or repeated patterns → escalate for review (don't pressure the customer).

## Customer-facing wording
> "That item isn't something you ordered, so it's yours to keep — no need to return it. Sorry for any
> confusion!"
