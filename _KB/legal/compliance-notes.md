# Compliance Notes (Agent Reference — Do Not Quote Verbatim)

> **Internal reference.** Summaries of the US laws that constrain our policies. Use them to decide
> the right action and to know when to escalate. **Do not read these statutes to customers** —
> respond with the plain-language, customer-facing wording in the topic articles. **Not legal
> advice.** State laws beyond California also apply (e.g., NY GBL § 218-a, FL § 501.142).

## 1. FTC Mail Order Rule (ship-time & refunds) — most important
- Applies to goods ordered by internet/phone/mail. You must have a **reasonable basis** for any
  shipping claim; with no stated time you must reasonably expect to ship within **30 days**. The
  clock starts at a **properly completed order**.
- If you can't ship on time, you must get the customer's consent to the delay **or promptly refund**
  for the unshipped merchandise.
- A **delay notice** must: give a revised date (or say it's unknown), offer to **cancel for a full,
  prompt refund**, and give a no-cost way to cancel.
- Refund timing for unshipped orders: **7 working days** for card/cash/check/money-order; one
  billing cycle if the seller extended the credit.
- **A gift card / store credit is NOT a refund** (FTC *Fashion Nova*). Required cancellation refunds
  must go to the original payment method.
- → Drives: [DELAY-003](../delays-and-compensation/delay-notices-and-your-right-to-cancel.md),
  [ORD-001](../order-management/cancel-an-order.md), [REF-004](../refunds/refund-timing-by-payment-method.md).

## 2. Fair Credit Billing Act (FCBA) — card disputes
- Consumer's written dispute must reach the card issuer within **60 days** of the first statement
  showing the error. Issuer **acknowledges within 30 days**, resolves within **two billing cycles
  (max 90 days)**.
- **Quality disputes are not "billing errors."** A chargeback is a bank process, distinct from our
  refund.
- → Drives: [PAY-004](../payments-and-billing/chargebacks-and-disputes.md).

## 3. Unordered merchandise — 39 U.S.C. § 3009
- Merchandise a customer never ordered **may be kept as a gift**. Do not demand its return. If we
  shipped an extra item in error, we may **ask** and offer a prepaid label — never require it.
- → Drives: [SHIP-009](../shipping-and-delivery/unordered-or-extra-items.md).

## 4. Magnuson-Moss Warranty Act — written warranties
- A written warranty on a consumer product over **$10** must be titled **"full"** or **"limited"**;
  terms must be available **before sale**.
- **Tie-in ban:** coverage can't require branded parts/service unless provided free. Never say
  "warranty void if repaired elsewhere."
- Implied warranties can't be disclaimed if a written warranty is offered.
- → Drives: [WAR-001](../warranty/warranty-overview.md), [WAR-002](../warranty/manufacturer-warranty-claims.md).

## 5. CCPA / CPRA — California privacy
- Rights: know, delete, correct, opt out of sale/sharing, limit sensitive PI, non-discrimination.
- **Confirm receipt within 10 business days**; substantively **respond within 45 calendar days**
  (one extension to 90 with notice). Honor **opt-out within 15 business days**. Offer at least two
  submission methods.
- Agent **routes** privacy requests to the privacy workflow; **never fulfills deletion in chat**.
- → Drives: [ACCT-003](../account-and-privacy/privacy-rights-access-delete-correct.md).

## 6. California Civil Code § 1723 — return-policy disclosure
- If a store does not offer full refund/credit/exchange for ≥ 7 days, it must **conspicuously post**
  its policy, or be liable for a refund on goods returned within 30 days. Exemptions: food, plants,
  perishables, "as is"/"all sales final," customized goods, and health-exclusion goods.
- → Drives: [RET-001](../returns/return-policy-overview.md), [RET-002](../returns/non-returnable-items.md).

## 7. Subscriptions / "click-to-cancel"
- The FTC click-to-cancel rule was **vacated July 8, 2025** and is **not in force**; the pre-2024
  Negative Option Rule was restored. State **auto-renewal laws (e.g., California) still apply**.
  Only relevant if {{STORE_NAME}} sells subscriptions.
