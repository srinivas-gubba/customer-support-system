# Marketing Email Opt-Out

**Summary.** Customers can unsubscribe from marketing emails via the **unsubscribe link** in any
marketing message or in **Account → preferences**. **Transactional** messages (order/shipping
updates) are separate and still sent. This is self-service and distinct from formal privacy rights.

## Rule details
- Unsubscribe = link in any marketing email, or account preferences.
- Transactional/order messages are **not** marketing and continue.
- Formal "don't sell/share my data" requests are a **privacy right**, not a marketing opt-out →
  [ACCT-003](privacy-rights-access-delete-correct.md).

## Examples
1. "Stop the promo emails" → unsubscribe link or preferences; order updates still arrive.
2. "I still got a shipping email after unsubscribing" → that's transactional, not marketing;
   expected.
3. "Don't sell my data" → route as a privacy opt-out (ACCT-003).

## What the agent must not do
- Do not stop transactional order updates as a "marketing" opt-out.
- Do not treat a legal opt-out as a simple unsubscribe.

## Escalation
Opt-out of sale/sharing or any formal privacy request → route to privacy (ACCT-003).

## Customer-facing wording
> "You can unsubscribe using the link at the bottom of any marketing email, or from your account
> preferences. You'll still get essential order updates."
