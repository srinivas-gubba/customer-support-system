# Warranty Overview

**Summary.** Warranties cover **manufacturing defects** under normal use for the stated period
(**default 12 months** from delivery; varies by product). They do **not** cover accidental damage,
misuse, or normal wear. Warranty is **separate** from the 30-day return window and from statutory
consumer rights.

## Rule details
- Period: **12 months** default from delivery; check the product/warranty page for specifics.
- Covers: manufacturing defects / failures under normal use.
- Excludes: accidental damage, drops/spills, misuse, unauthorized modification, normal wear,
  consumables.
- Magnuson-Moss (agent reference, [LEGAL-001](../legal/compliance-notes.md)): written warranties over
  $10 are labeled **"full"** or **"limited"**; **never** say "warranty void if repaired elsewhere"
  (tie-in ban); implied warranties aren't disclaimed when a written warranty is offered.

## Warranty vs. return vs. arrival-defect
| Situation | Path |
|---|---|
| Faulty on arrival / within 30 days | [DMG-004](../damaged-or-wrong-items/defective-on-arrival.md) (refund/replace) |
| Defect appears later, within warranty | [WAR-002](manufacturer-warranty-claims.md) |
| Misuse/accident | Not covered; possible paid repair |

## Examples
1. Laptop defect at month 5, normal use → warranty claim (WAR-002).
2. Cracked screen from a drop → not covered (accidental).
3. Dead on arrival → arrival-defect flow (DMG-004), not warranty.

## What the agent must not do
- Do not promise coverage for accidental/wear damage.
- Do not use "void if serviced elsewhere" language (illegal tie-in).

## Escalation
Statutory-rights claims, coverage disputes, or high-value items → escalate.

## Customer-facing wording
> "Our warranty covers manufacturing defects for 12 months from delivery. It doesn't cover
> accidental damage or normal wear, but if it's a defect, I can start a claim."
