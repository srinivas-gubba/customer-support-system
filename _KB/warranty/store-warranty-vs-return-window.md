# Store Warranty vs Return Window

**Summary.** The **30-day return window** (change of mind / arrival issues) and the **warranty** (12
months, manufacturing defects) are **different protections**. A request past 30 days isn't
automatically dead — if it's a defect, the **warranty** may still apply.

## Rule details
- Return window: 30 days from delivery, any reason within condition rules ([REF-001](../refunds/refund-eligibility-and-window.md)).
- Warranty: 12 months, **defects only** ([WAR-001](warranty-overview.md)).
- IF past 30 days AND a defect → **warranty path**, not a refund decline alone ([REF-006](../refunds/refunds-outside-window-decline.md)).

## Examples
1. Day 12, change of mind → return window (refund/exchange).
2. Day 90, defect → warranty claim (WAR-002), even though returns are closed.
3. Day 90, change of mind, works fine → neither applies → polite decline (REF-006).

## What the agent must not do
- Do not tell a customer with a month-5 defect that "returns are closed, nothing we can do" —
  consider warranty.
- Do not route a change-of-mind, past-window request to warranty.

## Escalation
Ambiguous defect-vs-wear, or high-value items → escalate.

## Customer-facing wording
> "Our 30-day returns are for change-of-mind or arrival issues; defects are covered separately by
> the 12-month warranty. Since this sounds like a defect, I can start a warranty claim even though
> it's past 30 days."
