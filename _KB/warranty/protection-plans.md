# Protection Plans (Service Contracts)

**Summary.** A protection plan is an **optional service contract**, separate from the manufacturer
warranty, that may cover things the warranty doesn't (e.g., accidental damage). Coverage, claims,
and cancellation follow the **plan's own terms**; the agent routes plan claims per those terms.

## Rule details
- A protection plan is **not** a warranty — it's a purchased service contract.
- Coverage (e.g., accidental damage, extended term) and claim steps come from the **plan terms**.
- Plan cancellation/refunds follow the plan's terms; escalate if unclear.
- Don't conflate plan coverage with the free 12-month warranty ([WAR-001](warranty-overview.md)).

## Examples
1. Customer dropped a plan-covered phone → file an accidental-damage claim per the plan terms.
2. "Is accidental damage covered by warranty?" → No, but a protection plan might; check if they
   bought one.
3. Cancel a protection plan → follow plan cancellation terms; escalate specifics.

## What the agent must not do
- Do not promise plan coverage you can't confirm in the plan terms.
- Do not treat plan claims as standard warranty claims.

## Escalation
Plan coverage/cancellation specifics, or disputes → escalate to the plan administrator/team.

## Customer-facing wording
> "If you bought the protection plan, accidental damage like this may be covered under its terms —
> let me check whether you have one and route your claim."
