# Manufacturer Warranty Claims

**Summary.** For a covered defect within the warranty period, collect **proof of purchase, a fault
description, photos/video, and the serial/model number**, then submit the claim. The outcome
(**repair, replacement, or refund**) is a **human/warranty-team decision** — the agent gathers
evidence and routes, it does not approve claims.

## Steps
1. Confirm within the warranty period and a covered defect ([WAR-001](warranty-overview.md)).
2. Collect: proof of purchase (order ID), fault description + when it started, **photos/video**
   ([DMG-005](../damaged-or-wrong-items/evidence-requirements-photos.md)), serial/model number.
3. Submit the claim for assessment; **escalate** the repair/replace/refund decision.
4. Covered claims: free remedy (prepaid label if return needed). Out-of-warranty/misuse: possible
   paid repair — confirm costs before charging.

## Examples
1. Tablet fault at month 4, photos + serial provided → gather and route; team decides remedy.
2. Out-of-warranty defect → offer paid-repair option; confirm cost first.
3. Overheating/smoking device → **stop**, advise stop use, escalate as safety.

## What the agent must not do
- Do not approve or deny a warranty remedy itself — escalate the decision.
- Do not charge for a repair without disclosing the cost.

## Escalation
Always escalate the claim decision; also escalate safety faults immediately.

## Customer-facing wording
> "Thanks — I have your order, the fault details, photos, and serial number. I'm submitting the
> warranty claim now; our team will confirm whether it's a repair, replacement, or refund and the
> timing."
