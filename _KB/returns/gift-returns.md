# Gift Returns

**Summary.** A gift returned by the **recipient** is refunded as **store credit to the recipient**
(not cash to the original buyer), within the standard **30-day** window and condition rules. The
original purchaser can instead be refunded to their payment method if they handle the return.

## Rule details
- Recipient return within 30 days of delivery, original condition → **store credit to the recipient**
  → see [PROMO-001](../store-credit-and-promotions/store-credit-terms.md).
- Purchaser-initiated return → refund to the **original payment method** ([REF-001](../refunds/refund-eligibility-and-window.md)).
- Protect privacy: do not reveal the purchase price or buyer details to the recipient beyond what a
  gift receipt shows; verify identity first ([ACCT-001](../account-and-privacy/verify-your-identity.md)).

## Examples
1. Recipient returns an unused gift sweater, 12 days after delivery → **store credit** to recipient.
2. Buyer wants to return the gift themselves → refund to buyer's original card.
3. Recipient wants cash for a gift → offer **store credit** (standard for gift returns), or buyer
   must process it.

## What the agent must not do
- Do not refund the buyer's card at the recipient's request without the buyer's involvement.
- Do not disclose the buyer's personal details to the recipient.

## Escalation
Value over $100, or a dispute between buyer and recipient → escalate.

## Customer-facing wording
> "Since this was a gift, I can set you up with store credit for the full value once we receive it —
> that way the original purchaser isn't involved. Want me to start that?"
