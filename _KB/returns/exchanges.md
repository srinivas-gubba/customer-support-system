# Exchanges

**Summary.** Exchanges follow the same **30-day window** and **condition** rules as returns and are
subject to **stock**. An exchange is handled as a **return plus a new order**: the customer pays any
price difference up, or is refunded a difference down. For store-at-fault items, the exchange is
free.

## Rule details
- Eligible within 30 days of delivery, item in original condition ([RET-003](return-condition-requirements.md)).
- Processed as a return + new order; the replacement is subject to availability.
- **Price difference:** higher-priced replacement → customer pays the difference; lower-priced →
  refund the difference.
- Store-at-fault exchange (wrong/damaged) → free, prepaid label → [DMG-006](../damaged-or-wrong-items/replacement-vs-refund-choice.md).
- Simple alternative: return for a refund and place a new order.

## Examples
1. Medium → Large, same $30 price, in stock → free swap of size (customer pays return shipping only
   if change of mind; often waived on even exchanges — follow store rule).
2. $30 top exchanged for a $45 one → customer pays the **$15** difference.
3. Desired color out of stock → offer refund or back-in-stock notification ([PROD-005](../product-specific-rules/apparel-and-footwear.md)).

## What the agent must not do
- Do not promise an exchange without confirming **stock** of the requested variant.
- Do not charge for an exchange when the store is at fault.

## Escalation
Replacement value over $100, or repeated exchange failures → escalate.

## Customer-facing wording
> "Happy to exchange that for a Large — it's in stock. I'll email a return label for the Medium and
> send the Large out. No price difference, so nothing more to pay."
