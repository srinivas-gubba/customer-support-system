# Non-Returnable Items

**Summary.** Some items cannot be returned for change of mind: **groceries/perishables, opened
beauty & fragrance, gift cards, personalized/customized items, pierced earrings & intimates,
installed vehicle parts, digital downloads, hazmat, pharmacy, final-sale, and opened software/media**.
A non-returnable item that arrives **damaged, defective, or wrong is still covered** under the
damaged-item policy.

## The exclusion list
- Groceries & perishables → [PROD-002](../product-specific-rules/groceries-perishables.md)
- **Opened** beauty, skin-care, fragrance (hygiene) → [PROD-001](../product-specific-rules/beauty-skincare-fragrance-hygiene.md)
- Gift cards and store credit
- Personalized / customized / engraved items
- Pierced earrings and intimate apparel/swimwear without hygiene liner → [PROD-006](../product-specific-rules/jewelry-watches-sunglasses.md)
- Installed vehicle/motorcycle parts and hazmat (fluids, loose batteries) → [PROD-007](../product-specific-rules/vehicle-and-motorcycle-parts.md)
- Digital downloads
- Pharmacy items
- Items marked **"Final Sale"**
- Opened software/media

## Key exception (do not override)
- IF the item arrived **damaged / defective / wrong / incomplete** → it is **still eligible** for a
  remedy regardless of this list → route to [DMG-001](../damaged-or-wrong-items/damaged-item-claims.md).
- Legal basis for conspicuous disclosure and the perishable/custom/health exemptions: CA Civil Code
  § 1723 → [LEGAL-001](../legal/compliance-notes.md).

## Examples
1. Opened perfume, change of mind → **not returnable** (hygiene).
2. Opened perfume that **leaked in transit** → **covered** as damaged (DMG-001).
3. "Final Sale" jacket, change of mind → **not returnable**; if defective → warranty/damaged flow.

## What the agent must not do
- Do not cite "non-returnable" to deny a **damaged/defective/wrong** remedy.
- Do not accept change-of-mind returns of excluded items — offer the correct path instead.

## Escalation
Allergic reaction or safety concern with a hygiene product, or a disputed exclusion → escalate.

## Customer-facing wording
> "That item is on our non-returnable list for hygiene reasons, so I can't take it back for a change
> of mind. If it arrived damaged or faulty, though, you're fully covered — just send me a photo."
